# PnP Bridge 1.10.4

The Day-N banner's device variables now fill from NetBox.

## New

- **`$device_name` and `$device_ip` resolve from NetBox.** Catalyst Center's own
  binding variables (`$__device.hostname`, `$__device.managementIpAddress`) are
  substituted by CCC and were printing literally in the banner. With the
  template switched to plain variables, `device_name` now fills from the NetBox
  device name and `device_ip` from its management IP.

  Also mapped off the same management address, for templates that need them:
  `MGMT_IP` / `MANAGEMENT_IP`, `MGMT_MASK` (dotted), `MGMT_PREFIX` and
  `MGMT_SUBNET` (CIDR).

  If the NetBox device has no primary IP, `device_ip` stays a manual field
  rather than resolving to something wrong.

## Upgrade notes

- No schema change. `git pull && make image`, recreate the container.
- Re-run "Resolve variables" on any job sitting on step 4.
