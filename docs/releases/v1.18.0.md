# PnP Bridge 1.18.0

## Fixed

- **One failing template no longer takes the rest of a composite down with it.**
  Deployment stopped at the first failing member, so a port template that tripped
  over an interface meant the VLAN and banner members never ran — the switch got
  neither. Every member is now attempted, and the failures are reported together:
  *"1 of 3 templates failed; the others were applied."*

## Added

- **Day-N can be split into two stages.** Step 4 deploys the base templates
  (VLANs, banner, anything safe); the new step 5, **Ports & uplinks**, deploys
  the port/uplink template afterwards.

  On the Day-N step, "Deploy Day-N" behaves as before and finishes the job.
  "Deploy, then configure ports" runs the base templates and stops short of
  activating NetBox, then offers "Continue to ports & uplinks". The device is
  only marked `active` in NetBox once the final stage succeeds — a device with an
  outstanding stage is not finished.

  Resuming a job whose devices are parked between stages lands directly on the
  ports step.

## Upgrade notes

- **Schema change**: migration `0012` adds `jobs.dayn2_template_id` and
  `job_devices.dayn2_variables`. Runs at startup; no manual step.
- `git pull && make image`, recreate the container.
- Single-stage Day-N is unchanged — the split is opt-in per run.
